CAT NeoVERSE · Vortex OT-Cloud

1U · Vortex OT-Cloud Edge Node

ARS-121L-NE316R

A dual-processor edge node bridging the OT-Edge to the Secure OT-Cloud.

  • 2 × Arm 16-core LX2160A
  • 8 × SFP+ 10GbE
  • 128GB DDR4 · 2TB NVMe
  • 2 × 300W PSU

Download Datasheet   Request a demo

The 1U Vortex node is the entry point to the OT-OCN fabric — a compact, Arm-based dual-processor chassis that runs the OT-Edge interface on one processor and the core OT-Cloud services on the other. Immune by design, deterministic by default.

Technical Specifications

Form Factor
1U 19" rackmount chassis, dual processor
Processor
2 × NXP Layerscape LX2160A — 16-core Arm Cortex-A72 @ 2GHz (Intel / AMD options)
Memory
64GB DDR4 DIMM per processor (128GB total)
Storage
64GB eMMC · 1TB M.2 NVMe SSD · 2 × SATA 3.0 — per processor
Networking
8 × SFP+ (10GbE each) · 2 × 1GbE copper (RJ45)
I/O
6 × USB 3.0 · 6 × USB 2.0
Power
2 × 300W PSU
Temperature
Commercial 0°C to 40°C
