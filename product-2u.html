CAT NeoVERSE · Vortex OT-Cloud

2U · Vortex OT-Cloud System

NEO-2U-X6700

A quad-Xeon OT-Cloud system with GPU acceleration and 24 NVMe bays.

  • Quad Xeon 6700 P-core
  • Up to 8TB DDR5
  • 24 × NVMe/SATA/SAS bays
  • 2 × double-width GPU

Download Datasheet   Request a demo

The 2U Vortex system scales the OT-Cloud into a four-processor platform — dedicating processors to OT-Edge, profile and cache services, application services, and GPU-accelerated OT-AI. Built for smart-city and finance workloads that demand real-time throughput.

Technical Specifications

Form Factor
2U 19" rackmount chassis, dual/quad processor
Processor
Quad Intel® Xeon® 6700-series (P-cores) up to 270W, air cooled; supports up to 2 double-width PCIe GPU accelerators
Memory
Up to 64 DIMMs — 8TB DDR5 in 2DPC
Storage
Up to 24 front hot-swap 2.5" NVMe / SATA / SAS bays
Expansion
Up to 2 × PCIe 5.0 x8 FHHL + 4 × PCIe 5.0 x16 FHHL + 2 × PCIe 5.0 x16 AIOM
Networking
Flexible — up to 2 OCP 3.0-compatible AIOM slots
Power
Redundant 2700W Titanium-level power supplies
Temperature
Commercial 0°C to 40°C
